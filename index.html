Frequently Asked Questions

What is Arctrix?

Arctrix is a futuristic, AI-driven technology brand focused on building deeply immersive and elegant products that solve real problems. Our flagship products include QuantC for secure file sharing and xWavy for AI audio enhancement.

How secure is QuantC?

QuantC utilizes state-of-the-art AES-256 end-to-end encryption and a zero-knowledge architecture. It operates entirely anonymously without logging user data or requiring accounts for transferring files up to 1GB+.

What does xWavy do?

xWavy is an AI-powered audio enhancement application for Android. It uses advanced machine learning algorithms to instantly isolate vocals and eradicate background noise, providing pristine, studio-grade sound for your recordings.
